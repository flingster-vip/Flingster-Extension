Welcome! Follow these simple steps to install the extension and activate your session.

---

## Step 1 — Download

1. Go to the releases page and download the latest `.zip` file
2. **Unzip** the downloaded file to a folder on your computer
   - Windows: Right-click → Extract All
   - Mac: Double-click the zip file

---

## Step 2 — Install in Chrome

1. Open Chrome and go to: `chrome://extensions`
2. Turn on **Developer Mode** using the toggle in the **top-right corner**
3. Click **Load unpacked**
4. Select the folder you unzipped in Step 1
5. The extension will appear in your toolbar (puzzle piece icon 🧩)

> **Tip:** Pin the extension for easy access — click the puzzle piece icon in Chrome toolbar → click the pin next to Flingster Member Access

---

## Step 3 — Activate your session

1. Click the extension icon in your toolbar
2. Enter the **access password** you received after booking
3. Click **Authenticate**
4. You'll see a green ✅ confirmation — you're now logged in!

## Troubleshooting

**"Invalid password"**
→ Double-check your password for typos. Passwords are case-sensitive.

**"Token has expired"**
→ Your access period has ended. Book a new slot to get a fresh password.

**"Access not yet active"**
→ Your access hasn't started yet. Check your booking confirmation for the start time.

**"Could not reach data store"**
→ Check your internet connection and try again.

**Extension not showing in toolbar**
→ Click the 🧩 puzzle piece icon in Chrome and pin the extension.

---

## Notes

- Your password is saved locally on your device if you check **Remember password**
- To log out, click **Clear Session & Cookie** inside the extension
- The extension only works on **flingster.com**
- The email and channel-password edit icons are hidden on Flingster, and open Flingster tabs refresh when the session expires
